Laminated Windscreen

By law, all modern vehicles are fitted with laminated glass. The windscreen for a vehicle is structural, so must be replaced correctly to ensure occupant safety. By law all glass fitted to any vehicle must meet all safety glass regulations. On average it may take 30mins to 1hrs to fit a windscreen correctly depending on the vehicle

Extra attachments on your windscreen

Some newer/more modern cars can have special features attached to the windscreen. These can include rain sensors, cameras(ADAS) and other types of sensors. Please let us know if you are aware of any attachments you may have, as this can increase the time required to get your vehicle safely back on the road. Please contact for help.

ADAS camera calibration after windscreen replacement

Some modern vehicles need their driver-assistance cameras calibrated after a windscreen change. Auto Mobile Glass can confirm what your vehicle requires and discuss the next step.

Why does calibration matter?

ADAS cameras support features such as lane keeping and emergency braking. Calibration requirements vary by make and model, so call with your vehicle details before relying on these systems after glass work.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you come to me?

Yes. Auto Mobile Glass is fully mobile across Whangarei, Ruakaka, Bream Bay and surrounding Northland areas. We will confirm a safe work location when you book.

How long does a windscreen replacement take?

Most replacements take around 30 minutes to 1.5 hours, depending on the vehicle and the glass required. We will give you a clear time estimate before starting.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We welcome insurance work and can help you through the claim details. Have your policy information ready when you call.

Can you repair a small chip?

Often, yes. A chip should be checked early before it spreads. Send us a photo or call 0800 367 264 and we will advise the best next step.

Where are your workshops?

You can find us at Unit 3, 17 Gumdigger Place, Raumanga, Whangarei, and 470 Marsden Point Road, Ruakaka. Call ahead to arrange an appointment at the location that suits you.

Do you replace side windows and other vehicle glass?

Yes. Alongside windscreens, we offer vehicle body glass replacement. Tell us which window is damaged and your vehicle details so we can check the glass required and arrange the work.

Do I need ADAS camera recalibration after a windscreen replacement?

Many modern vehicles need their ADAS cameras recalibrated after a windscreen replacement, because even a few millimetres of difference in the new glass position can affect lane-keeping, forward collision and other camera-based safety systems. Auto Mobile Glass checks whether your vehicle needs calibration as part of the job and can carry it out for late-model vehicles, something not every Northland glass company offers.

Can you provide a quote for Work and Income?

Yes. We can provide quotes for Work and Income. Contact our team with your vehicle details and the work needed, and let us know you require a Work and Income quote.

How much will my glass repair or replacement cost?

Contact us for a quote for your vehicle and the glass work required. Let us know whether you need a repair or replacement and whether you plan to make an insurance claim.

Will a cracked windscreen fail a WOF in New Zealand?

Often, yes. A crack anywhere inside the critical vision area directly ahead of the driver will fail a WOF, and cracks elsewhere on the windscreen fail once they pass about 150mm or the glass is otherwise too damaged to see through safely. If you are not sure whether your damage will pass, Auto Mobile Glass can check it and advise whether a repair or full replacement is the safer option.
