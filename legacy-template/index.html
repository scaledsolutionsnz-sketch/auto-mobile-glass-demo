The calibration step people miss

Why does ADAS calibration matter after a windscreen change?

ADAS cameras support features such as lane keeping and emergency braking. If your vehicle requires calibration after glass replacement, the calibration must be completed accurately before relying on those systems.

Auto Mobile Glass can confirm whether your vehicle needs calibration and discuss the next step. Equipment and vehicle coverage should be confirmed for your specific model.
